Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026.

Travel Gap (travel-gap.netlify.app) is a small, independently run road trip guide site. This page explains what data the site touches and what it doesn't.

What this site collects

Travel Gap doesn't have user accounts, forms, comments, or newsletters. Nothing you type goes anywhere, because there's nowhere on this site to type anything.

Hosting is provided by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for operating and securing the site. This site doesn't add its own analytics or tracking scripts on top of that.

Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Loading a font makes a request to Google's servers, which may log that request the same way any web request is logged.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site point to Auto Europe, a car rental comparison service, and are marked as affiliate links where they appear. Clicking one takes you to Auto Europe's site, which sets its own cookies under its own privacy policy, not this one. Travel Gap may earn a commission on bookings made through those links, at no extra cost to you.

Travel Gap's Pinterest account

Travel Gap publishes to its own Pinterest business account (@travel-gap) using the Pinterest API, to automate posting content this site's operator creates. This connection is used only to manage that one account. It does not read, collect, or share data belonging to any other Pinterest user, and no Pinterest user data is stored on this site.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Travel Gap Pinterest account.
