About Travel Gap

Travel Gap maps specific road trips in detail: which direction to drive, which stops are worth the time, and what to sort out before you go. Every route here is built around real, checkable geography, not generated lists or invented itineraries.

The site is new and growing one route at a time. If you've driven one of these roads and something here is wrong or missing, that's worth knowing. Get in touch.
