Cross-border fees, restricted car classes, and license rules catch more first-time European road trippers than bad weather ever does. None of it is complicated once you know to look for it. It just has to happen before you're standing at a rental counter, not during.

Before you book: 5 things to check

  1. Cross-border fees
    Driving between countries can add $50-100 at pickup, if it's allowed at all. Not every rental company permits it.
  2. Restricted car classes
    Premium brands are often banned from crossing into certain countries. Worth checking if you've booked something upmarket.
  3. Manual is the default
    Automatic transmission is less common in Europe, costs more, and books out faster in high season.
  4. Toll stickers
    Austria and Switzerland require a vignette bought before you drive, not at the border.
  5. Your license
    Whether you need an International Driving Permit depends on your home country and your license's alphabet, not just which country you're driving in.

Every one of these is a question of which company and which terms, not something you can check once and forget, because it changes by provider and by border. Checking five separate rental sites for five separate answers is most of the actual work.
